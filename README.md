# Product Performance & Returns Analysis (Power BI)

## Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard analyzes product sales performance, units sold,
product ratings, return behavior, return reasons, and category-level
return rates.
<img width="1117" height="600" alt="PP" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/41ac2594-cb9f-4572-93ba-ecd8e4e26ce7" />

The goal is to help a business identify which products generate the most
revenue, which products sell the most units, where returns are
concentrated, and which product categories may require attention.
## Dashboard KPIs
- Total Products: 15
- Total Units Sold: 4,126
- Top Product Revenue: $35K
- Product Return Rate: 9%
- Average Product Rating: 4.0
- Returned Orders: 256

## Business Questions
1. Which products generate the highest revenue?
2. Which products sell the most units?
3. Which product categories have the highest return rates?
4. What are the main reasons for product returns?
5. Which products are frequently returned?
6. How does revenue change across product categories and months?
7. How do customer type and region affect the analysis?
   
## Key Insights Visible in the Dashboard
- Leather Weekender Bag is the top product by revenue at
  approximately $35K.
- Slick Slip is the top product often retured.
- Cashmere Crewneck Sweater and Organic Cotton Sheets are
  among the products with the highest unit sales.
- Apparel has the highest displayed category return rate at
  10%.
- The overall product return rate is 9%, with 256 returned
  orders.
- The dashboard shows rating and return behavior together, making
  it easier to investigate products that may need attention.

## Business Value
This dashboard supports product and operations decisions by connecting
revenue, sales volume, product ratings, and returns in one view. A
business can use it to identify high-performing products, investigate
return-heavy products, and prioritize product or customer-experience
improvements.
